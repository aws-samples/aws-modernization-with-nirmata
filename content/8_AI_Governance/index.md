## AI Workload Governance on Amazon EKS with Agentic AI and Kyverno

In this section, you will secure and govern a simulated AI inference workload running on Amazon EKS using Kyverno policies generated by Nirmata's agentic AI assistant (`nctl ai`).

Because this hosted workshop environment uses standard compute instances, we use a lightweight mock AI inference service instead of a GPU-backed model. The governance patterns demonstrated here — resource limits, health probes, node placement, and cost guardrails — apply directly to production AI/ML workloads including GPU-backed inference environments.

### Why AI Workload Governance Matters

AI workloads present unique governance challenges:
- **Unbounded resource consumption** — a single misconfigured model can consume all cluster resources
- **Lack of observability** — without health probes, failed inference pods go undetected
- **Cost explosion** — GPU and high-memory instances are expensive; uncontrolled scheduling leads to waste
- **Security exposure** — AI pods with unnecessary API access expand the attack surface

### What You'll Do

1. Verify your environment and create a dedicated AI namespace
2. Use `nctl ai` to generate Kyverno policies from natural language
3. Block a non-compliant AI workload and observe the denial
4. Use `nctl ai` to explain why a workload was denied
5. Deploy a compliant AI inference workload and test it
6. Enforce cost guardrails to cap resource requests
7. Review policy reports in the Nirmata dashboard
